What Ellena Fit Coaching is

Real results.Made for life.

Ellena Fit Coaching is designed to help women build strength, lose fat and feel confident in their bodies, without restrictive diets or spending hours in the gym. Everything is fully personalised by Ellie, Lizzy & Ana.

There are 1000s of delicious recipes, nutrition tracking and weekly check-ins, so you'll have everything you need to reach your goals, all in one place. No more starting over every Monday, cutting out your favourite foods or wondering what to do in the gym. Just a realistic, sustainable approach to fitness that fits around YOUR lifestyle.

The Process

Your journeystarts here.

Getting started with Ellena Fit is simple. From your first application to your personalised coaching plan, we're here to guide you every step of the way.

  1. 01

    Apply for Coaching

    Complete our quick 2-minute application and tell us a little about yourself, your goals and what you're currently struggling with. This helps us understand how we can best support you.

  2. 02

    Let's Have a Chat!

    Once you've applied, you'll be directed to WhatsApp to chat with Ellie personally. We'll discuss your goals, answer any questions and give you an insight into how our coaching works to make sure it's the right fit for you.

  3. 03

    Let's Get You Started!

    Ready to join? You'll complete our more detailed onboarding process and set up your payment. We'll then use all the information you've provided to create a fully personalised coaching plan tailored to your goals, lifestyle and preferences.

  4. 04

    Your Transformation Begins!

    Over the next 12 weeks, we'll work together to help you achieve results while building the knowledge, confidence and healthy habits to maintain them long-term. Most of our clients choose to continue with us on a flexible monthly rolling basis after their initial 12 weeks, allowing them to keep progressing and build results that truly last.
