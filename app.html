FAQs

Questions?We've got you.

Is the app suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. Whether you've never stepped foot in a gym or you've been training for years, the plans are clear and easy to follow.

Can I train at home?

Yes. There are plans for the gym and for home, so you can train wherever suits you.

Do I have to follow a strict diet?

Never. You'll have daily targets to work towards and 1000s of recipes to choose from, but you can still enjoy the foods you love, eat out and socialise.

How is this different to 1:1 coaching?

The app gives you Ellie's workouts, recipes and tracking tools to follow on your own. 1:1 coaching adds a fully personalised plan, weekly check-ins with your coach and 24/7 WhatsApp support. You can upgrade to coaching at any time.
